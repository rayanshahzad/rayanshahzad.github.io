About

Hi, I'm Rayan.

Mechanical engineer from UET Lahore, finishing an MSc at KAUST in December 2026. I design and build electromechanical systems end to end: the mechanical and fluidic hardware, custom PCBs, embedded firmware and the software around them.

Before KAUST I spent two years improving production at British American Tobacco and Ingredion, where every change was judged by measured OEE, waste and cost. I bring that habit to everything I build: make the rig, instrument it and test it against numbers. I also wrote a novel, The Tangerine Dusk.

Touchless pilot

Industrial automation · BAT Pakistan

Touchless production pilot

Took people out of the control loop in primary manufacturing: conditioning, cutting and drying. As Project Process Lead I mapped every manual touch, shaped the architecture and specifications with the system integrator and drove delivery through installation and start up.

  • One redundant SCADA control room across 21 work centres, Rockwell and Siemens
  • Dryers retrofitted so measured moisture feeds the PID loops directly
  • Specified, tested twice (FAT and SAT) and started up on a 31 week plan
  • Introduced mean time between touches as a metric next to MTBF and MTTR

Elexint

Mechatronics · Elexint · Cofounder and CTO

Eight channel gravimetric fragrance dispenser

Doses by measured mass instead of pump run time, so tubing wear and viscosity changes never creep into the blend. I designed the PCB, wrote the firmware and built every machine.

  • Closed loop interface with custom designed firmware and hardware
  • Custom designed PCB incorporating multi channel inputs
  • Per pump drip offsets, an 800 ms settle and a reverse purge between channels
  • 200 dispense cycles logged, working toward ±0.1 g on every pump

Hydro turbine

Fluid machinery · ERU lab group project

Eight jet hydro turbine in PVC

Built to recover energy from water already flowing through the lab. I led machine design, blade FEA and fabrication, and brought in Beta Pipes to sponsor all of the material.

  • Stepped PVC manifold feeding 8 jets into an 8 blade runner
  • Voltage rose from 1.2 V to 4.6 V as jets closed at fixed flow (R² 0.996)
  • Next: load, speed and head readings for a full efficiency curve

Water generator

Thermal systems · BSc final year project, UET

Atmospheric water generator

A vapor compression machine that pulls water out of humid air, built from refrigeration parts on a welded steel frame. My part was the mechanical design and hardware integration.

  • R134a hermetic compressor, capillary expansion and a finned evaporator coil
  • Instrumented with a thermocouple, anemometer and hygrograph
  • Two 12 hour outdoor runs in Lahore at 66 W input
